NovaGate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

NovaGate (“the game”) is a multiplayer 3D space game.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the username and password you choose when you register. Passwords are stored in hashed form on our server.
  • Gameplay data: in-game progress, scores and actions needed to run the multiplayer game.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide the game: to sign you in, save your progress and run multiplayer features. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Third parties

We do not share your data with third parties, and the game contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing eylemaylinackln@gmail.com. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
